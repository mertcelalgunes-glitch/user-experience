Our Story

From a small school supply shop to the leading educational retailer serving students nationwide.

Who We Are

EduMart was founded in 2010 by a group of educators who saw a need for affordable, high-quality school supplies that didn't compromise on quality. What started as a single store near a university campus has grown into a nationwide online retailer serving millions of students each year.

Our mission is simple: to provide students with the tools they need to succeed in their education at prices that make sense for student budgets. We believe that access to quality supplies shouldn't be a barrier to learning.
