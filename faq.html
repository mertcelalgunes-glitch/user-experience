We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for most items. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. To initiate a return, please contact our customer service team with your order number. Some specialty items may have different return policies which will be noted on the product page.

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website. If you're having trouble tracking your package, our customer service team is happy to help.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and Apple Pay. We also accept purchase orders from schools and educational institutions with approved credit accounts.
